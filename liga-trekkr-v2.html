02

Win your matches

No complicated scoring. Your rank is decided by your results, in this order:

1W–L
Win – Loss
Most wins ranks highest.
2H2H
Head-to-head
Tied on wins? Whoever won when you faced each other ranks higher.
3PD
Point difference
Points won minus points lost.
4PF
Points for
More points won is better.
5PA
Points against
Fewer points conceded is better.
