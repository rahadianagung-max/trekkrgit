Where your data comes from

Only matches you actually play at a Trekkr partner venue or community count. You register or claim your profile once in the player app; from then on, the venue host records your results — you never enter your own scores.

  • Ranked Play sessions at partner venues
  • Tournaments & leagues in the network
  • Trekkr Series & the Championship

From match to rating — 4 steps

  1. You play a match at a partner venue or community.
  2. The host records the score — captured live through the TurnamenPadel scoring tools, or submitted as final results.
  3. Trekkr computes your ELO from the result, your opponents' ratings and the margin.
  4. Your passport & the national board update automatically — see it in the app.

What Trekkr stores

Just what's needed to compute your rating and rankings:

  • Your name / display name and profile (photo, region, IG — optional)
  • Matches you played: partners, opponents, scores, venue & date
  • Your rating history and tier
