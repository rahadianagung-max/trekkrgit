TREKKR is a performance rating platform for padel players that uses the globally recognized Elo Rating System. Unlike traditional skill levels that rely on subjective assessments, TREKKR calculates ratings from actual match results — making every match measurable and meaningful.

How TREKKR works

Elo-based rating engine

TREKKR uses the same rating methodology originally developed for chess and now widely used across competitive sports. Your rating changes after every recorded match based on:

  • Your current rating
  • Your opponents' ratings
  • Match outcome (win or loss)
  • Margin of victory

The system continuously updates your rating to reflect your current playing strength.

Dynamic team-based calculation

Padel is played in pairs. TREKKR calculates the average rating of each team and compares it against the opposing team. The system then:

  1. Estimates each team's probability of winning.
  2. Evaluates the actual result.
  3. Adjusts ratings accordingly.

This creates a fair and accurate reflection of player performance over time.

Calibration period

New players need match data before receiving a reliable rating.

  • Minimum 15 recorded matches
  • Continuous participation improves accuracy
  • More matches = more reliable rating

As additional results are recorded, the system becomes increasingly precise in measuring player ability.

Margin of victory matters

Winning is important — but how you win also matters. A dominant victory demonstrates stronger performance and may generate additional rating benefits compared to a narrow win.
