How Trekkr works

Play. Get rated.
Climb.

Trekkr turns every match you play into a single, fair rating — and gives clubs the tools to run the sessions behind it.

01
Play a match anywhere
League night, tournament, or a casual Mexicano at any Trekkr venue. Your score gets recorded.
02
Your ELO updates
A rating system (like chess) adjusts your number based on who you beat and who you lost to — not just win or lose.
03
One rating, every venue
The same number follows you everywhere, so levels are comparable across the whole community.
04
Climb the leaderboard
Weekly PlayRank, badges, and your passport track your progress — and unlock deeper analytics.
