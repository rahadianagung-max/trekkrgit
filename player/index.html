Calibration Phase
How Trekkr finds your real level
1
You start at the floor of your level
New players are entered at the lowest ELO of their starting band. You begin at the bottom of the band you were entered in, then prove you belong there.
2
Win to stay, lose and you drop
Because you start at the floor, the level is a test you must pass. During calibration your ELO moves faster (higher K-factor): win and you hold your level and climb; lose consistently and your ELO falls below the floor, automatically dropping you to the level below — so you settle where you truly belong.
3
Minimum 15 matches to calibrate
15 recorded matches gives the engine enough data to place you accurately. This typically takes 2–3 Mexicano sessions. Until then, your rating is an estimate — not a verdict.
4
Then you become Rated
After 15 matches, your Calibrating badge becomes Rated. Your ELO is now a trusted reflection of your skill. The tier you land in — T3, T2, T1 — is earned, not assigned.
After calibration is complete
Your ELO rating is considered accurate and trustworthy
Your tier badge changes from Calibrating to Rated
ELO movements become more stable — rewarding consistency
You appear in national and venue rankings with full credibility
