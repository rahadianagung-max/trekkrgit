Calibration Phase
How Trekkr finds your real level
1
Everyone starts at 1350
All new players begin at ELO 1350 — the entry point of Lower Bronze. This ensures every first match is fair, regardless of your actual skill level.
2
The engine watches your results
During calibration, your ELO moves faster (higher K-factor). Win consistently and you rise quickly. Lose often and you settle lower. The algorithm is actively finding your true level.
3
Minimum 15 matches to calibrate
15 recorded matches gives the engine enough data to place you accurately. This typically takes 2–3 Mexicano sessions. Until then, your rating is an estimate — not a verdict.
4
Then you become Rated
After 15 matches, your Calibrating badge becomes Rated. Your ELO is now a trusted reflection of your skill. The tier you land in — Bronze, Silver, Gold — is earned, not assigned.
After calibration is complete
Your ELO rating is considered accurate and trustworthy
Your tier badge changes from Calibrating to Rated
ELO movements become more stable — rewarding consistency
You appear in national and venue rankings with full credibility
