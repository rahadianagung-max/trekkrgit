Tournament Formats

Choose The Format That Fits Your Community

Playoff Championship
Round Robin → Playoffs

The most familiar and widely used format. Players or teams compete in group-stage round robins before advancing to knockout playoffs.

Double Path Championship
RR → Upper & Lower Brackets

Designed to maximize competitive balance. After the round robin, players split into Upper and Lower Brackets based on performance — meaningful competition for every level.

Community League
Multi-Week Series

A structured league played across multiple weeks. Teams or players accumulate points throughout the season before advancing to championship rounds.
