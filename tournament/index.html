Tourney & League

Every Result Feeds The Real Level

Trekkr tracks the players in your tournaments and leagues to build one trusted player database — so organizers and players can always see everyone's real level. It's an anti-sandbagging system: no more hidden ringers, no more players sneaking into a bracket far below their skill. Every result you send makes the next draw fairer for everyone.

Two Ways We Track Your Event

Whether your competition is live or already finished, there's a simple path to get its results into the Trekkr database.

1 · Automatic Tournament Tools

Run your event on our automatic tournament engine, turnamenpadel.com. Scores are captured live and flow straight into the Trekkr database as matches finish — no spreadsheets, no double entry, no extra admin for your team.

Explore turnamenpadel.com →
2 · Send Us Your Final Results

Already wrapped up? Just hand over your final match data as a spreadsheet (Google Sheet or Excel) and we'll import it into the database for you. Submit through the Get Listed form and our team will take it from there.
