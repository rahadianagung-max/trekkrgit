About

Built for the Indonesian
padel community

Trekkr started as a simple leaderboard and grew into a full platform for players, clubs, and communities — because the scene deserved one fair, shared way to measure the game.

Our mission

Give every padel player one rating that travels with them, and give organizers the tools to run better, fairer, more connected sessions.

Why it matters

The community fragments into closed circles. A shared, honest ranking is what lets separate groups finally play — and grow — together.

What's next

Deeper analytics, community leagues, and tools that make running padel in Indonesia effortless.
