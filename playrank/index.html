PlayRank

Competitive Play. Meaningful Progress.

PlayRank transforms casual games into structured competitive experiences that keep players engaged, returning, and improving. Whether you're looking for your next ranked session or building a thriving padel community, PlayRank provides flexible competition formats designed for every level of play.

Two Ways to Compete

Weekly PlayRank
The Weekly Competition Engine

A recurring ranked competition played throughout the week within a single venue or community. Players participate in multiple sessions, earning wins and points along the way. At the end of the week, the player with the strongest overall performance claims the top spot.

PlayRank Combine
Discover Your Community's Competitive Landscape

A structured PlayRank format designed to accurately identify player levels and create a clear competitive hierarchy within a community. Unlike traditional social events, Community Skill Index focuses on generating a large sample of meaningful matches to reveal true player performance.
