What is Trekkr

Where Every Match Builds Your Journey

Trekkr is a padel rating platform for Indonesia. Play at a partner venue or community, and every match you play builds one national rating that follows you everywhere — no more guessing who's actually at your level.

Get the Player App
Built for real players

Whether you play casually every week or chase tournaments, Trekkr gives you a fair, verifiable level and a place on the national board.

One rating, everywhere

Ranked play, tournaments and leagues at any venue in the network all feed a single ELO rating and tier.

Fair & anti-sandbagging

Your level comes from results, not self-assessment — so brackets and matchups are honest for everyone.

Your passport, in your pocket

Track your ELO, tier, best partner, badges and match history in the free player app.
