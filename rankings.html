How Rankings Work

From Match To Board In Five Steps

  1. Play a recorded match at any Trekkr venue or community.
  2. The engine compares the result to each pair's expected outcome.
  3. Your ELO rating moves — more for upsets and dominant wins.
  4. After 15 matches your rating is calibrated and board-ready.
  5. Men's and women's national boards update automatically.
